BrickPHY doesn't play samples. It makes the sound the way a real instrument does: something sets a body ringing. You choose how it is played, with a bow, with air or with a mallet, and what rings: a string, a bar, a plate, a bell.

I loved the physical modelling synths of the 90s, and now the Brick is strong enough to do it in your pocket. The sound engine is Elements by Mutable Instruments, the open source code by Émilie Gillet.

I'm testing the first version on my Brick now. It's not ready to download yet: follow @brickworld_hub on Instagram to know when it's out.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01Choose how it is played: a bow, air (blow) or a mallet (strike). Or mix all three.
  2. 02Choose what rings: the shape of the body, how bright it is, how long it rings, where you play it, and how much space around it.
  3. 03Play it from a MIDI keyboard, with velocity and pitch bend, or from the Brick's own buttons. Hear it on the Brick's jack or on a USB audio card.

No samples, no loops: every note is played by the model, so it never sounds the same twice.

WHAT IT DOES

BOW, BLOW, STRIKE

Three ways to play the sound, each with its own level and colour. A short strike, a slow swell or a long held note.

THE BODY

From a string to a bar, a plate and a bell. Brightness, damping, the point where you play it, and a stereo space with reverb.

16 SOUNDS

Eight to start with: cello, glass bell, wind pipe, pluck, kalimba, bowed metal, breath drone, marimba. Change them and they save by themselves.

WORKS WITHOUT MIDI

Play eight scale notes on the D-pad and the A B X Y buttons. Hold makes the last note keep playing, good for bowed and blown sounds.

JACK OR USB OUT

Out on the Brick's headphone jack, or on a USB audio card. You can switch while it plays.

SAME FAMILY

Same dot-matrix screen, same buttons and the same kind of C audio engine as the other BrickWorld apps. With a live oscilloscope.

NO BRICK YET?

BrickPHY will run on the TrimUI Brick, a small handheld console, with KNULLI on its SD card. KNULLI is free: download it and follow the install guide at knulli.org. The app will be free: the Brick is the only thing you need to buy.

  • 3.2" screen, 1024 × 768
  • USB-C for audio interfaces, USB synths and MIDI keyboards
  • Headphone jack out
  • 3000 mAh battery, 159 g
  • White, black, red or purple
