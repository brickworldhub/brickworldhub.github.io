BrickCompanion doesn't play on its own. It works next to your analog synth. The Brick makes one extra sound and you send it with a cable into the synth: into the external audio input as a sub oscillator, or into the mod input to make metallic, FM-like tones. Your synth's filter and envelopes do the rest.

I'm testing it now on my own synth. It's not ready to download yet: follow @brickworld_hub on Instagram to know when it's out.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01Run a mono 3.5 mm cable from the Brick's headphone jack to your synth's external audio input or mod input.
  2. 02Pick what the Brick sends: a sub oscillator under your notes, or a modulator at audio rate.
  3. 03Play. With MIDI it follows your keyboard. Without MIDI it plays a fixed note, a drone, or the notes you play on the Brick's buttons.

One voice, one signal, one cable. Mono only, on purpose: a mono cable uses just the left channel, so that's where the sound goes.

WHAT IT DOES

SUB OSCILLATOR

One or two octaves under your note, or the same octave and one up. Sine, triangle, saw, square or pulse with width. Into the external input it goes through your synth's own filter and VCA.

MODULATOR

Into the mod input: a wave that follows your note at a ratio (1:4 up to 7:1), or a fixed frequency from 20 to 2000 Hz. Bells, metal, growls.

WORKS WITHOUT MIDI

MIDI makes it follow your keyboard, but you don't need it. Fixed note for drones, or play eight scale notes on the Brick's buttons.

OSCILLOSCOPE

See the exact wave leaving the Brick, live, on the dot-matrix screen. It stands still on a held note.

TUNING

An A-440 reference tone, master tune, fine tune and glide, so it sits right with your synth.

SAME FAMILY

Same screen, same buttons and same C audio engine as the other BrickWorld apps.

NO BRICK YET?

BrickCompanion will run on the TrimUI Brick, a small handheld console, with KNULLI on its SD card. KNULLI is free: download it and follow the install guide at knulli.org. The app will be free: the Brick is the only thing you need to buy.

  • 3.2" screen, 1024 × 768
  • USB-C for audio interfaces, USB synths and MIDI keyboards
  • Headphone jack out
  • 3000 mAh battery, 159 g
  • White, black, red or purple
