BrickCompanion doesn't play on its own. It works next to your analog synth. The Brick becomes an extra bank of three oscillators, with its own arpeggiator, and you send it with one cable into the synth's external audio input. Your synth's filter and envelopes do the rest.

I'm testing it now on my own synth. It's not ready to download yet: follow @brickworld_hub on Instagram to know when it's out.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01Run a mono 3.5 mm cable from the Brick's headphone jack to your synth's external audio input.
  2. 02For MIDI, use your synth's MIDI THRU: keyboard into the synth, synth THRU into a USB MIDI interface on the Brick. Both play the same notes.
  3. 03Set up the three oscillators, or turn on the arpeggiator, and play. No MIDI? It plays a fixed note, a drone, or the notes on the Brick's buttons.

One voice, one signal, one cable. Mono only, on purpose: a mono cable uses just the left channel, so that's where the sound goes.

WHAT IT DOES

THREE OSCILLATORS

Like the oscillator bank of a classic three-oscillator mono synth. Range Lo, 32', 16', 8', 4', 2', a mix level for each, and OSC 2 and 3 tuned up to 7 semitones up or down.

WAVES

Triangle, shark, reverse saw, saw, square, wide and narrow pulse, sine. OSC 3 can follow the keyboard or stay on a fixed note, for drones and low hums.

ARPEGGIATOR

The keys you hold, or a chord from one key (major, minor, 7ths, sus, power fifth, or your own). Up, down, random, as played, 1 to 4 octaves, gate, swing, latch. Internal tempo or MIDI clock.

WORKS WITHOUT MIDI

MIDI makes it follow your keyboard, but you don't need it. Fixed note for drones, or play eight scale notes on the Brick's buttons.

OSCILLOSCOPE

See the exact wave leaving the Brick, live, on the dot-matrix screen. It stands still on a held note.

TUNING

An A-440 reference tone, master tune, fine tune and glide, so it sits right with your synth.

NO BRICK YET?

BrickCompanion will run on the TrimUI Brick, a small handheld console, with KNULLI on its SD card. KNULLI is free: download it and follow the install guide at knulli.org. The app will be free: the Brick is the only thing you need to buy.
