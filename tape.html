BRICKTAPE
Features Controls Download Next

A 4-track recorder for the TrimUI Brick · KNULLI · v1.1.2

Four tracks.
Ten minutes.
One cassette.

BrickTape is a small four-track recorder, like the cassette four-tracks of the 80s and 90s. Record up to four tracks, punch in to fix a mistake, then mix them. On the screen the reels turn while the tape plays.

Download v1.1.2 User manual (PDF)

Free and open source (GPL-3.0) · Source code on GitHub

FEATURES

FOUR TRACKS

Four mono tracks. Record one at a time, or two at once from the left and right inputs.

TEN MINUTES OF TAPE

A song can be up to 10 minutes long. It all stays in memory while you record, so nothing waits for the SD card. The reels show how much tape is left.

PUNCH IN AND OUT

Press record while the tape plays to fix one part. The punch lands where you heard it, with a short crossfade so there is no click.

SAFE, REC, MUTE

Each track has three states, changed with SELECT: safe from recording, ready to record, or muted.

REWIND AND FAST FORWARD

Hold the button: it starts slow and speeds up to 30 times faster, you hear the tape winding, and it stops right where you let go. Find the spot by ear.

UNDO THE LAST TAKE

Not happy with what you just played? Take it back, or bring it back again.

MIX PAGE

For each track: input (left, right or both), volume, pan and mute.

METRONOME

A click from the start of the tape, to play in time. It is never recorded.

USB OR MIC

Record from a USB audio interface, a USB mic or a synth with USB audio. With a two-channel interface, two tracks can record two different inputs at once. Or use the built-in mic: while it records the sound stops, a limit of the Brick.

SAVED IN THE PROJECT

Your tracks are saved as WAV files with the project, together with where the tape was.

CONTROLS

  • APlay, or punch out
  • XRecord, or punch in on the tracks set to REC
  • BStop, press again to go back to zero
  • YPause
  • L2/R2Hold to rewind / fast forward, let go to stop
  • SELECTTrack state: SAFE, REC, MUTE
  • Y+XUndo / redo the last take
  • Y+BClear the track
  • L1/R1Change page: TAPE, MIX, FX, SET

DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL

Download BrickTape 1.1.2 User manual (PDF)

BrickTape_v1.1.2.zip · Free, GPL-3.0. The zip has the full source inside. The user manual (12 pages, PDF) shows every page and setting, with pictures. Want to read the code first? See the source on GitHub.

First, your Brick needs KNULLI, a free system you put on the SD card. Get it and its install guide at knulli.org.

  1. 01Download the zip and unzip it on your computer.
  2. 02Copy BrickTape.sh, the .keys files and the bricktape folder to roms/ports on the SD card. Updating? Copy over the old files: your projects stay.
  3. 03Start BrickTape from Ports. The first time it unpacks a few files, which takes a few seconds. Not in the list? Update the games list in the KNULLI menu.

Choose your input in SET > SOURCE. Hold SELECT for the controls. If something goes wrong, bricktape/log.txt says why. Tested on KNULLI Scarab.

NO BRICK YET?

BrickTape runs on the TrimUI Brick, a small handheld console, with KNULLI on its SD card. KNULLI is free: download it and follow the install guide at knulli.org. The app is free: the Brick is the only thing you need to buy.

  • 3.2" screen, 1024 × 768
  • USB-C for audio interfaces, USB synths and MIDI keyboards
  • Headphone jack out
  • 3000 mAh battery, 159 g
  • White, black, red or purple
