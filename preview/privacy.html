PRIVACY AND COOKIES

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This is the website of BrickWorld, a small set of free music apps for the TrimUI Brick, made by Alessandro Pietrosanti. I want to keep this simple and honest: I collect as little as possible.

WHO I AM

Alessandro Pietrosanti, United Kingdom. You can write to me at info@brickworldhub.com.

GOOGLE ANALYTICS

If you click "Accept" on the cookie message, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits. It tells me things like:

  • which pages people open and for how long
  • the type of device and browser
  • the country or city (roughly, not your address)
  • how you found the site, for example from Google or Instagram

I use this only to understand what people find useful. I don't use it for ads and I don't sell any data. Google Analytics puts cookies in your browser (their names start with _ga) and the data is kept for up to 14 months. Google processes it for me; you can read Google's privacy policy.

If you click "No thanks", Google Analytics doesn't run at all and no analytics cookies are set.

OTHER THINGS THE SITE LOADS

  • Fonts come from Google Fonts. To send them, Google sees your IP address, like any website you visit. No cookies.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep your IP address in its logs for security.
  • Your cookie choice is saved in your own browser, so the message doesn't come back on every page.
  • Links to other sites (Ko-fi, Instagram, GitHub, and the shop link to buy a Brick) take you to sites with their own privacy rules. The shop link is an affiliate link: the shop may set its own cookies when you visit it, so it knows you came from here.

EMAIL

If you write to me, I use your email only to reply. I don't add you to any list.

YOUR RIGHTS

Under UK data protection law you can ask what data I have about you, ask me to correct it or delete it, and say no to it being used. Just email me. If you're not happy with my answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
