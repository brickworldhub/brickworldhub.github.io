A loop station for the TrimUI Brick · KNULLI · v1.5

Play it.
Loop it.
Stack it.

BrickLooper is a five-track loop station for the Brick, made for playing live. Plug in a USB audio interface, a USB mic or a synth with USB audio, or use the built-in mic. Record a loop, stack more on top, and every track stays in time with the first one.

Download v1.5.0 What it does

Free and open source (GPL-3.0) · Source code on GitHub

FEATURES

FIVE STEREO TRACKS

Record, overdub, stop and play each track on its own, with its own volume and level meter. Up to 60 seconds per track.

ALWAYS IN TIME

The first take sets the loop length. Every other track snaps to it and becomes 1, 2 or 3 loops long.

EASY ON TIMING

Close a take a little early and it keeps recording to the end of the loop. Close it a little late and the extra bit goes on top of the start.

USB OR MIC

A USB audio interface, a USB mic or a synth with USB audio, from channel 1, 2 or both. Or the built-in mic, good for quick ideas: while it records the sound stops, but the loop keeps time.

METRONOME

Click on or off, 40 to 240 BPM, in 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8 or 7/8. With the click on, the first loop is a whole number of bars. The click is never recorded.

COUNT-IN

Press A, count along with one bar of click, and play on the next beat 1.

ADD OR REPLACE

Overdub adds a layer. Replace records over the old part where you play, and undo brings the old part back.

UNDO AND REDO

Took a wrong overdub? Take it back, or put it back again.

FX ON THE OUTPUT

Drive, bitcrush, filter, delay and reverb on everything you hear. Your takes are recorded clean. No clicks when you switch an effect on or off.

SAVED WITH THE PROJECT

START saves your loops with the project. When you open it again they are there, stopped and ready. B starts them.

CONTROLS

Hold SELECT to see them on the screen.

  • ARecord, then overdub, then play, on the track you picked
  • XStop or start the track
  • BStart or stop all tracks
  • Y+AUndo / redo
  • Y+XClear the track, then A to record it again
  • Y+BClear everything (it asks first)
  • UP/DOWNPick a track (L2 / R2 work too)
  • LEFT/RIGHTTrack volume

DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL

Download BrickLooper 1.5.0

BrickLooper_v1.5.0.zip · Free, GPL-3.0. The zip has the full source inside. Want to read the code first? See the source on GitHub.

First, your Brick needs KNULLI, a free system you put on the SD card. Get it and its install guide at knulli.org.

  1. 01Download the zip and unzip it on your computer.
  2. 02Copy BrickLooper.sh, the .keys files and the bricklooper folder to roms/ports on the SD card. It sits next to BrickSampler and doesn't touch it.
  3. 03Start BrickLooper from Ports. The first time it unpacks a few files, which takes a few seconds. Then choose your input in Source on the LOOP page.

Hold SELECT for the controls, MENU + START closes the app. If something goes wrong, bricklooper/log.txt says why. Tested on KNULLI Scarab with a USB synth and a USB audio interface.

NO BRICK YET?

BrickLooper runs on the TrimUI Brick, a small handheld console, with KNULLI on its SD card. KNULLI is free: download it and follow the install guide at knulli.org. The app is free: the Brick is the only thing you need to buy.

  • 3.2" screen, 1024 × 768
  • USB-C for audio interfaces, USB synths and MIDI keyboards
  • Headphone jack out
  • 3000 mAh battery, 159 g
  • White, black, red or purple
