A loop station for the TrimUI Brick · KNULLI · v1.0

Play it.
Loop it.
Stack it.

BrickLooper is a five-track loop station for the Brick. Record loops from a USB audio interface or a USB synth, from the pads and notes of the built-in sampler, or from both at once. Every track stays in time with the first one.

What it does Controls

v1.0 · Built on BrickSampler, with all of it inside · USB input still being tested on the Brick

FEATURES

FIVE STEREO TRACKS

Record, overdub, stop and play each track on its own, with its own volume.

ALWAYS IN TIME

The first take sets the loop length. Every other track snaps to it and becomes 1, 2 or 3 loops long.

EASY ON TIMING

Close a take a little early and it keeps recording to the end of the loop. Close it a little late and the extra bit goes on top of the start.

RECORD FROM ANYWHERE

The USB input, the sampler's pads and notes (after the FX), or both together. You can hear the input while you play.

UNDO AND REDO

Took a wrong overdub? Take it back, or put it back again.

BACK TO THE TOP

When everything is stopped and you press play, all loops start again from the beginning.

SAVED WITH THE PROJECT

START saves your loops with the project. When you open it again they are there, stopped and ready. B starts them.

THE WHOLE SAMPLER

Everything from BrickSampler is inside: kit and chromatic modes, loops and chop, LFO, FX, MIDI and the REC page.

LATENCY OFFSET

The delay of the output and the USB input is taken out for you. If a take still sounds a bit early or late, move Offset to fix it.

CONTROLS

Hold SELECT to see them on the screen.

  • ARecord, then overdub, then play, on the track you picked
  • XStop or start the track
  • BStop or start all tracks
  • Y+AUndo / redo
  • Y+XClear the track
  • Y+BClear everything (it asks first)
  • UP/DOWNPick a track (L2 / R2 work too)
  • LEFT/RIGHTTrack volume

INSTALL

BrickLooper has its own folder, next to BrickSampler. It doesn't touch your BrickSampler projects.

  1. 01Download the latest release. [LINK]
  2. 02Copy the bricklooper folder and its launcher to roms/ports on the SD card.
  3. 03Launch BrickLooper from Ports. It opens on the LOOP page: pick the Source and record your first loop.

For USB, plug in an audio interface or a USB synth like the FM-1. The built-in mic is for sampling only: the Brick can't record from it while the speaker plays. Tested on KNULLI Scarab.
